About AYMERE

Built for
better decisions.

Real questions.
Deeper answers.
A brighter tomorrow.

Independent thinking. Quantitative depth. Clear communication.

AYMERE is an Amsterdam-based data and intelligence firm helping organisations understand complex business questions through rigorous analysis.

We combine quantitative expertise with commercial understanding to uncover what drives performance, communicate what the evidence means and help decision-makers determine what to do next.

Our approach is deliberately focused: stay close to the question, stay close to the data and stay close to the people making the decision.

Our principles

Rigour

Conclusions should withstand scrutiny.

Clarity

Complexity should never become an excuse for poor communication.

Curiosity

The right question often matters as much as the right model.

Impact

Analysis has value when it improves a decision.
